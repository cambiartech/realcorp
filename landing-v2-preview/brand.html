Palette

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the height of the R's counter (the notch in the bowl) on all four sides — roughly ⅛ of the tile.

Minimum size

16px for the tile mark on screen, 6mm in print. Below that, use the mono variant — the copper leg muddies at small sizes on coloured stock.

Don't recolour the leg

The copper leg is the only accent. If a single colour is required, use mark-mono-black or mark-mono-white, not a tinted variant.

Don't set the wordmark in another face

Instrument Sans SemiBold at −0.03em tracking. Nothing else.
