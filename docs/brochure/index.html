The desks02

Projects

The thing you sell has one home.

A development is a list of units. Each unit has a price, a status, and a person it belongs to once it is sold. That is the stock. Everything else in the company hangs off it.

The project

The estate or the building, with the units under it. One place to open when someone asks what you have.

The unit

Available, reserved, or sold. The price agreed. The client on the file. You can see which doors are still open without calling the site.

The client

The buyer’s record: who they are, which unit, and the papers that belong to that sale. Import a list you already have, then stop keeping a second one.

What they still owe

A deposit, a milestone, a service charge. The contract value minus what has been collected is the balance. It updates when finance receipts the money.

Shown in public

What you mark for the public page is what a buyer can see. The internal status stays on the project.

The same unit, let

A unit you let short-stay is still that unit. The sale record and the stay record do not invent two apartments.

If your work is not a building or a unit, this desk can stay quiet. The leads, the money, and the people still have a place.

Projects03

Leads and deals

Someone owns the buyer.

A name in a group chat is not a pipeline. A lead has an owner, a source, a temperature, and a next step. When it is real, it becomes a deal on a unit, and it cannot be called won until finance has looked.

New Contacted Qualified Inspection booked Inspection done Negotiation Reservation Won Lost

Where they came from

A form, a campaign, an ad, WhatsApp, or a partner. The source stays on the lead, so the month’s numbers have a parent.

Warm, and a score

Quality and a score, so the desk knows who to call first. The score moves when the deal moves.

An owner

The lead is assigned. The activity log shows who spoke to them and when. A buyer does not sit with everybody and therefore with nobody.

The deal is the unit

A deal can sit on a specific unit. The value is the value. Invoices and sales receipts hang off that deal, so the close and the money are the same story.

Finance sees the close

A deal can wait on finance. They approve it or they send it back. Won means the company agreed, not that a salesperson typed the word.

Lost is still a record

A lost deal stays, with the stage it died on. You can see what you are losing, and you do not delete the month to make the board look clean.

Leads and deals04

Lead forms

They fill it at night. You have a lead by morning.

Put a form on your site, in a bio, or on a flyer. The person gives you a name, a phone, the project they want, and a budget. That submission is a lead. Nobody copies it out of an email.

Start from a shape that already works

A lead form with name, email, phone, project, and budget. A short contact form. An event list with who they are. Or a blank form you build field by field.

The fields a sale actually needs

Name, email, phone or WhatsApp, a dropdown, a number, the project they are asking about, the budget they named. You choose what is required.

A link, a page, a QR

Each form has a public link. Drop it in Instagram, WhatsApp, or a signature. Embed it on a site you already have. Print the QR on a banner at the gate.

You know which post sent them

The link can carry the campaign, the ad, and the partner. When the lead arrives, that trail is already on it. You can see which flyer, which realtor, which post.

Marketing holds it, then releases it

An entry can sit with marketing while you check it. When you let it through, sales sees a lead with the answers in the notes. Sales is not drinking from a raw spreadsheet.

You can see if the form is working

Who opened it, who finished it, on a phone or a laptop, and what hour they came. A form that nobody completes is a form you can fix.

A finished form can also open a WhatsApp chat with the answers already written, so the first reply is a reply, not a hunt for the message.

Lead forms05

Marketing

Every enquiry has a parent.

A campaign, an ad, a WhatsApp chat, a public listing. They should all arrive as leads you can count. Marketing is the desk that makes that true.

Campaigns

Name the push: the launch, the open house, the month on radio. Leads from that push stay tied to it, so you can say what the spend brought back.

Facebook and Instagram

Lead ads come in on their own. The name and the number are already a lead, with the campaign on them. You stop downloading a file from the ads manager to find out who raised a hand.

WhatsApp, on the lead

The conversation sits on the person, both ways. Your team replies from the lead. A listings bot can answer the first questions: what you have, where it is, what it costs.

A public page in your name

Explore is your listings page. Share it, or embed it on a site you already own. What a stranger sees is what you published from the project. An enquiry from that page is a lead, ready to become a stay or a sale.

Entries, before they are leads

Form fills land in marketing first. You can see the raw entries, then release the ones sales should work. The rest do not clutter the pipeline.

One source of “where from”

Campaign, form, ad, partner, WhatsApp, Explore. The lead carries one of those. The Monday meeting stops being a debate about whose buyer it was.

Marketing06

Short Lets

Sell the night once.

If you let apartments, this is the hotel desk inside the company. The building, the flat, the guest, the dates, and the bill are one stay. A night that is taken cannot be taken again.

Locations and apartments

The estate, the flat, the photos, the nightly rate, the beds, the baths, how many guests. This is the inventory the channels will read.

The room board

Today: who is in, who is due out, which rooms are still dirty. Housekeeping and the desk look at the same board.

Front desk

Check a guest in. Check them out. The day is visible from the desk, not reconstructed from chats at 6pm.

Reservations and guests

The booking, the name, the dates. A guest who has stayed before is the same guest, with a history, not a new row every Friday.

Inspections

Walk the apartment before they arrive and after they leave. The condition is written down while you are standing in the room.

The guest bill

Nights and extras, posted on the stay. What they paid goes to Finance as short-let income, on that apartment, in that period.

Calendars from the other sites

Airbnb and Booking.com busy dates come in. Realcorp blocks those nights. You do not discover a double booking when two guests arrive with bags.

Close the day

The night audit shuts the business day. Reports say what the rooms earned. The number is the number finance will see.

Companies that do not let rooms leave this desk off. It does not have to be in the setup.

Short Lets07

Finance

If it was sold, it can be receipted. If it was receipted, it is in the books.

The deal, the stay, and the bank should not each have their own version of the truth. Finance is where the company agrees on a figure, and keeps the paper that proves it.

What you are owed

Receivables from the agreements on file. A client deposit and a milestone installment count toward the sale. You can see what is still open on that unit.

What you owe

Payables, in the same workspace, so money out is not a different company from money in.

Invoices

Draft, sent, part-paid, paid, or void. A payment lands on the invoice. The balance is what is left, not what someone believes is left.

Sales receipts

The buyer gets a receipt tied to the payment and the deal. It can go out by email, with the mark of something received, and the PDF is the same payment finance recorded.

Short-let income, apart from the sale

Money from a stay is earnings on that apartment. It is not treated as a buyer paying down a purchase. Service charges sit in their own line too.

Expenses, banking, remittances

What you spent, which account it moved through, and the tax and pension amounts finance has to file. Reports can split income by project and by short let.

VAT the way the invoice needs it

None, on top, inside the price, or exempt. The document matches the treatment you chose.

Who touched the figure

Documents live on the file. An audit log says who changed what. A close that finance rejected stays rejected until someone deals with it.

Finance08

Investors

Give them a login that shows their stake, and nothing past it.

People who put money in, or who own a listing you operate, should not have to call you for a PDF. They sign in. They see what is theirs. Your pipeline, your payroll, and everyone else’s balance stay with you.

My portfolio

The projects they are in, what they put in, and how collections on that project sit against their share.

My short lets

Apartments they own and you let. The stays, without handing them the front desk, the other owners, or the night audit.

My documents

The papers that belong to them, opened by them, when they want them.

Stakeholders, on your side

You record who holds what on a project. The portal is filled from that record. You are not assembling a personal report the night before a meeting.

One person can hold a stake in more than one organization. They choose the company, then they see that company’s share of their record.

Investors09

Tasks

The work has a name on it and a day it is due.

Follow-ups, inspections, site jobs, the thing someone promised in a meeting. If it lives in a chat, it dies in a chat. A task has an owner, a space, and a due date, and it can sit on the deal or the stay it belongs to.

Spaces

A board for the company, and boards for the teams that do the work. People land in the space that matches their desk, and they can still see what is theirs.

Assigned, not announced

A task goes to a person. They get the mail. The title is the title. It is not a message that fifty people were copied on.

It repeats until you stop it

Weekly inspections, monthly follow-ups, the jobs that are the job. Mark one done and the next one is already there. End the series when the work is over, or stop it early.

On the thing it is about

A task can belong to a deal, a project, a stay. Open the work and the context is there. You are not asking “which flat was this again?”

My work

Each person can see what is on them. A manager who is allowed to can see the company’s board. Front desk sees the work they were given.

Done means the next one can start

Closing a repeating task creates the next due date. Deleting one occurrence is not the same as killing the series. You choose.

Tasks10

Facility

Know where the cement went.

Sites consume materials, machines break, and somebody has to say what was used on which unit. Facility is the yard: stock, usage, plant, and damage, with the suppliers and artisans you actually buy from.

The catalog

What you keep: materials, with a name and a class. One list for the company, not a different list per foreman.

Stock, by place

What is on hand, and at which site. Receive a delivery and the balance moves. You can see a week of movement when something does not add up.

Usage

Drawn for a project or a unit. The material left the store and landed on the job, so the cost of that job is not a guess at the end.

Plant

The machines, and the next date they are due for service. The reminder exists before the machine stops in the middle of a pour.

Damages

What broke, where, and when it was written. A crack found on inspection has a place to live besides a photo on someone’s phone.

Suppliers, artisans, prices

Who you buy from, who you hire on site, and what the price was last time. The next order starts from the last price, not from memory.

No yard, no store, no site: leave Facility off. The rest of the company does not require it.

Facility11

People

The corporation is the people who keep it.

Hire them properly. Know who they report to. Pay them from the same record finance trusts. And on the morning of their birthday, let the company be the one that remembers.

The offer

Send an offer they can open and accept. The letter is the start of the file, not a PDF that never meets the payroll.

The first week

The forms a new hire has to finish, on a link, in their own time. What they submit lands on their record. HR is not chasing scans in a chat.

The org chart

Who reports to whom, drawn from the line on each person. Move someone, and the chart moves. You do not redraw a slide.

Leave

A request, an approval, a balance. The dashboard can show who is away. The work of the week is planned with that in view.

Appraisals

The review happens on their record. The form checks itself before it submits. A review is not a Word file named “final final”.

Pay, tax, and pension

Payslips from the person file. Earnings, deductions, and what is pensionable. Tax and pension schedules finance can file, from that same run. Lagos PAYE is the path the product knows.

Their own door

My HR: the person sees their leave, their documents, their payslip. They do not need the HR manager’s login to know what they were paid.

The morning it is their day

A birthday, and a work anniversary, sent in the company’s voice, and shown on the dashboard the same morning. The company that wants to last should not depend on someone remembering.

People12

Community

Pay the person who brought the buyer.

Agents, realtors, and referrers are how a lot of companies actually sell. Give them a roster, put their name on the lead, and let the closed deal remember them when it is time to pay.

A roster, not a contact list

Each partner is a record: who they are, and how many leads they have sent. You can see the bench.

Their name rides the lead

A form link can belong to a partner. A lead can name them. When that lead becomes a deal and the deal is won, the partner is still on it.

Payout from the close

What they are owed follows the deals they closed. The month-end argument starts from the record, not from a WhatsApp voice note.

A door of their own

A partner gets a link and a portal. They see the work they brought. The company’s other buyers, books, and staff stay on your side of the door.

Who actually produced this month

A board of partners, counted from leads and from deals that closed. The loudest person in the meeting is not automatically the top of the list.

Same buyers as sales

Community does not keep a private list. The lead sales is working is the lead the partner sent.

Pellows

She booked the flat from the chat.

On a Friday evening, someone needs a place for the weekend. She does not install another app. She opens WhatsApp and asks. What comes back is the flat: the photos, the nightly rate, and the nights that are still open. She holds the dates and pays, still in that conversation.

Nothing she saw was typed in a second time. The apartment, the pictures, and the price were already on the short-let desk in Realcorp, where the front desk already knows who is in the room. Pellows is the product that carries that room out to her.

The rooms stay yours until you turn Pellows on. When you do, it may read the apartments, the photos, the rates, and the nights already taken. Your books stay closed. Your people stay closed. A night sold on Airbnb or Booking.com stays sold, so the chat cannot give that bed to someone else.

She can be shown the rate you already charge. Or you can add a percentage, and that percentage stays on each time the room changes in Realcorp, so a new price does not wipe what you added. When you turn Pellows off, her search loses the rooms. The board your desk works from does not move.

Pellows is the first product we are setting beside Realcorp. The corporation remains the corporation. More products will come out of it, and this is the one that starts that line.
